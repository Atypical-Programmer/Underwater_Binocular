## Interpretation

duplicated or anomalous point-cloud derivatives, raw depth/image caches, and
rebuildable SfM feature/database artifacts. This baseline does not by itself
authorize deletion: each candidate must be classified, summarized, checked for
source inputs and uniqueness, and recorded in the delete or archive manifest
before any destructive operation.

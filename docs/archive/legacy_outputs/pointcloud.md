# Point-cloud archive index

The custom SDK point cloud is the selected reference product because its metadata verifies the PLY layout, pose replay, mapping index, and calibration file. Custom GEN_1 and low-provenance point clouds are comparison evidence; their large derivative PLY files are not retained as active products after the cleanup manifest records their hashes and reasons.

See `validation/reference/20260802_150233/calibration_reference.json` for the calibration convention and the local archive READMEs for exact original/new paths.
